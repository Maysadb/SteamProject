📖 Project Mission & Engineering Story

❓ 1. The Real-World Problem

Wind speeds fluctuate constantly throughout the day. When wind slows down or stops completely, standard wind turbines stop generating power and connected lights turn off.

✨ 2. The Smart Solution (Windy Bulb)

We designed a physical wind turbine model paired with a Digital Twin Simulator. It tests optimal wind locations and integrates a smart backup battery system that automatically engages during calm weather so the bulb glows 24/7.

☀️ 3. Wind Energy vs. Solar Energy

Solar panels depend on sunlight and stop generating power at night. Wind turbines can operate both day and night, making wind power a vital partner for continuous clean electricity!
